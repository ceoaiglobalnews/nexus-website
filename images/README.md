# Images Folder

## Upload Your Profile Photo

**Required:** Upload your professional headshot photo to this folder.

### File Details:
- **Filename:** `bruno-profile.jpg`
- **Recommended size:** 400x400px to 800x800px
- **Format:** JPG or PNG (rename to .jpg)
- **Aspect ratio:** Square (1:1) works best
- **Quality:** High resolution professional headshot

### How to Upload:

1. Save your headshot photo as `bruno-profile.jpg`
2. Upload it to this `images` folder in your repository
3. The photo will automatically appear in the "About" section of your website

```

```

Once uploaded, your photo will appear in the "About" section with a modern design and gradient border effect.
