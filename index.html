Thought Leadership

Insights on the future of digital healthcare operations

NHS England Board | June 2025

The NHS Needs Digital COOs & CEOs

A call to action for NHS leadership on bridging the gap between digital strategy and operational delivery.

"The hardest part of digital transformation isn't buying technology-it's changing how work is done. This is fundamentally a people and leadership challenge, and right now, the question to who owns it."

Key Points:

  • The Leadership Gap: Technology boards focus on delivery. Operational boards focus on performance. But who owns adoption, embedding, and making technology actually improve patient care?
  • Single Patient Record Transformation: SPR could be one of the most patient-centred transformations in NHS history—but only with the right operational leadership to translate national ambition into everyday service change.
  • The Digital COO and CEO Roles: Someone who is digitally literate, operationally credible, persuasive enough to bring teams together, and determined enough to see implementation through.
  • The Evidence: The Darzi review calls for a major tilt towards technology. The National Audit Office warns that transformation is held back by skills, capability, and the difficulty of embedding new ways of working locally.
